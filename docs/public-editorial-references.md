# Public editorial references

Owner rule: the site explains findings and practical implications itself. Internal source notes, competitor-research rationale, unverified discussion threads and capture indexes are not reader-facing copy. Article references default to internal unless explicitly approved as substantive research or an organiser's operational information. Source labels remain factual; never print note metadata. Retain original source inventory and attribution for editorial maintenance. Historical facts and practical advice remain on the page. Uncertain dates or rules remain explicitly uncertain, and official entry, route, assessment and safety instructions remain available. No new evidence or verified archival snapshot is claimed.

This change is a rendering boundary, not a confidentiality mechanism: static client modules and repository history may still contain internal research data. Do not store secrets in catalogue metadata.
