# Rucking Revolution — content and product standards

**Status:** Consolidated working standard, 25 November 2026. Applies to the v2 repository `ruishinor/ruckrev-revisited`. Based on retrieved owner instructions and the PR #34 baseline. New recommendations are identified as such. Supersedes conflicting incremental guidance, not source records or owner-approved content. Do not publish this internal document as site copy.

## 1. Mission and audience

Ruck with purpose. People | Purpose | Higher Standards. Learn, Train, Lead. Serve the first-time rucker, the experienced endurance participant, the person preparing for a demanding event or formal occupational assessment, and the person developing as a crew member or leader. The site should help people find something meaningful to work towards, prepare, act, and learn from it. Remain civilian, inclusive, independent of commercial organisers, and credible. Ambition and accessibility coexist; do not equate challenge with compulsory suffering, military imitation, or arbitrary weights. Preserve all useful existing content and functionality; correct unsupported claims without erasing useful history.

Every page should answer: Who is this for? What does it add beyond a search result or link? What can a reader do next? What limits or uncertainties matter? If the page has no independent value, deepen it, combine it with a relevant page, or retain it as a clearly labelled research lead; do not pretend that a skeletal entry is a completed resource. Existing public URLs require explicit migration/redirect review before removal.

## 2. Language and editorial voice

- English, natural and common conversational language. Use terminology appropriate to rucking and endurance. No clinical, corporate or performatively tactical voice; no generic AI motivational filler. Do not use the word “evidence” in ordinary public RR copy (owner preference). Use “ruck” where it is genuinely rucking; “walk”, “walking”, occupational names such as vessel operator, and official titles are valid when accurate. Do not distort facts to satisfy a word ban.
- Prefer concrete examples, meaningful instructions and measurable observations over generic disclaimers or platitudes. Make cautions specific to the activity; avoid repeated blocks of identical text. Do not overpromise readiness, safety or qualification.
- Keep original approved first-person narratives verbatim; typographic corrections only when authorised. Clearly distinguish real accounts, attributed third-party summaries, organiser-selected testimonials, historical reports and fictional practice cases. Never present a summary or invented narrative as a firsthand account. No invented sources, dates, quotes, photographs, endorsements or research provenance.
- No public development-history, original-repository, credits/brand-material sections or internal research notes. Retain legitimate source attributions, image rights and legal notices. Approved reference copy is not a development credit.
- Company inspiration: use original synthesis rather than reproducing proprietary programmes. Owner preference is not to clutter the public copy with routine company inspiration citations; retain internal source logs. Public attribution is required when using another party's specific claims, quotations, images, formal test rules, third-party accounts or material whose licence requires it. Do not hide necessary provenance.

## 3. Research and source policy

Primary publisher/organiser or governing authority first; actual edition rules, official registration, manuals and forms before summaries. Use source-language search and accurate translated terminology; distinguish translation from original. Cross-check differences instead of silently resolving incompatible sources. Record each material claim against the exact source, document version/page/section, date actually inspected, jurisdiction/role/intake/edition, and outstanding ambiguity. “Checked” means the content was inspected, not a URL responded. Secondary coverage can establish context; Reddit/forums can document experience or lead to sources, not official requirements. Cite original thread/comment where relevant; APA7-compatible references for confirmed externally sourced material, including test and event pages. Do not duplicate identical bibliography links. Link archives where useful: a wildcard index is a discovery link, never proof of a capture. Verify actual timestamp and content before calling a historical snapshot verified.

Keep unknown fields null or visibly unknown, never infer mandatory load, support, eligibility, exact dates or distances. If sources conflict, show each scoped claim with its date. Historical events stay accessible, separate edition rows from future confirmed dates. Corrections should log the reason and source, not erase historical context. Network failures, 403/405/429 and redirects are review signals rather than closure or deletion. No rate-limit circumvention. Use modest bounded manual checks; do not depend on remote requests in CI. Review confirmed future events about every 30 days, other/historical entries and assessments about every 180 days or on publisher revision; prioritise time-sensitive/official changes. These are suggested maintenance intervals already present in catalogue documentation, not tests of editorial quality.

## 4. Page-specific definition of done

### Independent challenges

A challenge must feel worth doing and have a distinct identity. Include meaningful objective, audience/prerequisites, format and target, realistic preparation and route/equipment choice, ordered execution, completion/adaptation/withdrawal criteria, what to record and compare, reflection and next steps, links to actual relevant workouts/plans/events, and optional printable/downloadable record/community invitation. Scale by ability and experience; add advanced goals without increasing multiple workload dimensions at once. Clearly distinguish self-reported completion from externally verified achievements. Maintain stable challenge ID and version; if targets change, old records retain original version and selected target. Existing two-option format remains a current interface contract, not a permanent cap on ambition. Target examples must be achievable for the intended audience and not coercive. Advanced challenges should add real substance (e.g. staged endurance, cumulative progress, navigation, team responsibility) rather than simply heavier packs. Include challenge-specific images where available and clearly label generated illustrations. Monthly rotation, sharing and My Training must use the same authoritative target.

Editorial review: Can someone plan, execute and review this without inventing the missing instructions? Does repeating or progressing have meaning? Does it differ materially from other entries? No word-count, paragraph-count or mandatory generic exercise count as a proxy for quality.

### Formal assessments and recruitment references

Identify authority, exact test name, applicable population, edition/date, official source(s), purpose and entry vs selection vs ongoing occupational distinction. Provide full published components, order, distances, unit definitions, load (dry/total/vest and extra equipment), time/repetition criteria, rest, scoring and invalid attempts where documented. Distinguish minimum, elevated/competitive and historical values. Provide accessible setup diagrams when appropriate (original vector diagrams, exact dimensions, clear scale/orientation, text equivalent; Illinois and Luc Léger are reference standards for usability). No invented protocol or implication that official entry thresholds guarantee selection. Independent preparation, example week, mistakes and log fields must be separate from official criteria. Use safe qualified supervision for aquatic and occupational apparatus; no independent underwater breath-holding prescriptions. Keep genuine recruitment-only entries clearly labelled and do not fake a test table where the protocol is unpublished; source-backed description still needs enough context to add value. Only enable comparison when like-for-like component definitions, direction, units and scope are defensible; do not treat a passed component as an overall pass. Reference-only cards are searchable; exact test assessment eligibility is deliberate, not a fixed number. Check all detail, card, comparison, preparation, and bibliography representations for consistency.

### Events and adventures

Organiser, place/country, event identity, date/edition and verification status; variants as coherent sets (distance, days, participation, load, support, terrain, qualification). Add event-specific history/tradition, route/terrain, logistics, participation and registration rules, gear/transport considerations and useful preparation based on verified facts. Mark estimates and unknowns. For external links use original organiser/registration and official sources. Separate event history, verified edition and research lead. Include relevant photograph of the actual event where licensed, with factual attribution. If only contextual photography is available, label it; do not imply event documentation. Link suitable programmes, workouts and actual participant accounts. Never invent an official route, GPX, dates, qualification or availability. International relevance extends beyond Norway, including civilian/military-tradition marches, occupational tests, adventure races and independent endurance routes.

### Training programmes, workouts and articles

Original accessible, progressive programming, not copied competitor products. Identify audience, starting capacity, purpose, duration, session frequency, equipment/terrain, example dose and recovery. Structured programmes need actual scheduled sessions, weekdays, progression and easier periods, options for interrupted training, and coherent prerequisites. No invented structured schedule for legacy narrative plans. Do not claim a short rehearsal guarantees race readiness. Explain adjustment, progression, intensity, load, nutrition, footwear, foot care, recovery and relevant constraints in context. Workouts require usable execution instructions, example dosage, rest and adaptations; articles should explain why and connect to real relevant event/assessment/report or training material. Maintain downloadable worksheets and import consistency. Review by suitably experienced practitioners for higher-risk or advanced content. Use natural headings; do not enforce exact number of sections or words.

### Crew, leadership and community

Provide real-world civilian practice: briefing, inclusion, mixed pace, shared load, group accountability, leadership handovers, communication, decisions, contingencies, service, sustainable community-building. Role practice must be voluntary and adjustable. Each drill has objective, task, preparation, constraints, review and follow-up; no automatic qualification or competence score. Directory entries require public sources and contact/status provenance; no unauthorised personal records. Distinguish active/historical/unconfirmed. Contribution templates remain manual/local until publication and privacy processes are defined; obtain permission and consent, agree attribution, verify factual claims and protect private details. Preserve real reports and separate practice cases.

### Routes, maps, tools and reading

Distinguish published route profiles from illustrative route templates. Give source, access, distance/ascent and date/conditions where known, map link, transport/exits, terrain and restrictions. Use local GPX processing with limits and safe parsing; no private track sent remotely. Native basemap work is optional pending licence/attribution/privacy decision. Tools require clear units, validation, output explanation and predictable error states; never silently infer load or readiness. Reading catalogue needs correct bibliographic identity, author/publisher links, useful explanation and rights review of cover use; unconfirmed titles not presented as verified books. Printable/digital guides should be genuinely useful standalone publications, not just repackaged navigation pages.

## 5. Imagery and visual system

Use original approved RR hexagonal mark, approved ruck/run silhouettes and reputable upstream icon set where applicable. Preserve brand red #8B0000; on dark surfaces use approved readable foreground red (e.g. #D87878) rather than unreadable dark red. Transparent red stencils, clear labels, no decorative icon for ambiguous category. No text outlines, repeated global photo backgrounds or repetitive empty card boxes. Use page-specific visual identity, meaningful visual hierarchy, comfortable readable width, whitespace, no horizontal overflow at narrow widths, accessible keyboard/focus and clear CTA. Learn/Train/Lead remain meaningful top-level routes; totals derive from catalogues, not manual copy. Assessment comparison remains available.

Prefer unique relevant real photographs when the subject is a real event, person or assessment. Source URL, creator, licence/permission, licence URL, attribution, caption, whether actual or contextual, original/derivative identity and permitted modifications are required in the internal asset log. Public attribution where required. Do not claim a generated illustration is an actual event. Do not substitute an unrelated exercise or agency photograph merely to remove a placeholder. If no appropriate reusable image exists, use clearly labelled original protocol diagram/graphic or request permission; report this as missing photographic coverage, not completion. CPAT remains unresolved. GIGN demonstration image is contextual; reassess FSK and other approximate matches. Where reuse is allowed, one thumbnail and matching detail for the same subject is acceptable; no reuse across unrelated subjects. Optimise local WebP derivatives with quality/dimensions appropriate to display, keep originals/provenance outside deployed assets as needed. Verify crop, focal point, contrast, visual accuracy and responsive decoding. Generated challenge illustrations must retain the real mark, clear disclosure and working downloads. Do not use identical artwork for multiple challenges.

## 6. Functional and privacy requirements

Preserve device-local IndexedDB workspace and explicit saves, import preview, backups, stale-tab protection, record revisions, migration and historical references. No new accounts, analytics, uploads, sync, external posting or data transmission without deliberate product and privacy review. JSON backups for restoration; CSV for analysis with formula protection. No implied encryption or durable backup. No hidden automatic deletion. Date targets are plans, not eligibility/registration. Keep completed records and adapted/stopped/deferred outcomes. Preserve CSP and response security policy; privacy claims must match actual deployed behaviour and provider processing. No legal compliance claims merely from having a policy. Verify cookies/storage/network requests, access requests and consent where relevant. Use minimal dependencies and do not weaken security for convenience.

## 7. Publication acceptance

One named accountable workstream owner; bounded change set; inspect existing implementation and sources first. New pages must pass structure, links/assets, meaningful human content review and responsive visual review. A source-backed factual correction must be checked in the published page, not merely data file. Automated checks cover invariants, not quality judgments. Retain protected data, assets and functionality; record intentional changes. Run risk-based checks while iterating; full final verification once on the exact candidate, hosted required checks and preview, then merge if mergeable and authorised. Verify post-merge main CI, production alias/commit and affected public routes. Report blocked items and what was actually observed. No “complete” status until criteria met. Internal documentation is not public content.

## Owner rule: editorial freedom

Copy changes must not be automated release blockers. Editorial voice, exact phrasing, attribution and narrative preservation belong in human editorial/source review. No hard-coded public-copy assertions, word blacklists or narrative checksums in CI. Structured factual standards, safety/data semantics and functional behavior remain protected.
