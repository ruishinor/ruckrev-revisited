## Competitive review — use as discovery, not feature copying

Prior conversations investigated GORUCK (participation and community/challenges), PATHFINDER (structured progression and challenge choice), SOFLETE (training depth and education), YOMP (ruck/hybrid formats), Green Beret Fitness (remote and tiered challenges and events), Spotter Up (editorial/practical human experience), ARWS (event discovery and formats), and related endurance diaries. Reuse broad ideas, not proprietary plans or content. Re-check current public offering only when it affects a specific implementation decision; this document has not rerun the full competitive web study. RR's differentiator is organiser-independent, civilian, inclusive access with connected preparation, local-first progress, credible source-linked assessments and real experiences. Do not pursue paywalls, mandatory membership, public leaderboards, social account requirements or extreme branding simply because competitors use them.
