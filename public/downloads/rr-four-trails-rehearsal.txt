Four-week mountain and transfer rehearsal

For people already comfortable on local trails. Pick a repeatable long route H on terrain you know. This is an equipment and navigation block, not preparation sufficient for a 55–126 km trail race.

Week 1: one easy 30-minute walk or run, one controlled step-down session, one familiar trail outing H.
Week 2: repeat the same duration and load. Practise a short climb and safe descent within H; note footing and gear access.
Week 3: keep H unchanged; practise a planned route junction and a timed public transport transfer on a separate easy outing. Allow buffer time.
Week 4: reduce long route time to roughly two-thirds of H; review descent comfort, layers, route instructions and the fallback finish.
