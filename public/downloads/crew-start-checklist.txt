Before launching
[ ] Agree on the purpose and participation options.
[ ] Choose a reliable meeting time and a short, accessible first route.
[ ] Check crossings, toilets, transport and shorter alternatives.
[ ] Decide how to communicate changes and cancellations.
[ ] Recruit a second person who can lead when you are unavailable.
[ ] Describe the session honestly: distance, pace, terrain and load options.
[ ] Plan how to welcome someone who arrives alone or without equipment.

On the first outing
[ ] Confirm the route and regroup points; introduce everyone.
[ ] Explain that load, pace and distance can be adjusted.
[ ] Check that the group remains together or has an agreed split plan.
[ ] Ask everyone what worked and what would make them return.

After four weeks
[ ] Review attendance without publishing personal details.
[ ] Rotate leadership and agree the next four dates.
[ ] Choose one improvement and assign a person to follow up.
