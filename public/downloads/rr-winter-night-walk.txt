RUCKING REVOLUTION — Eight weeks for a winter night walk

Starting requirement: repeatable, comfortable 90-minute walks. This is a rehearsal and capacity block, not proof of 52 km readiness. Choose an easier route or extend preparation when necessary.

Week | Easy walk / strength / long walk (minutes) | What to rehearse
1 | 30 / 25 / 80 | Lighting and layers in daylight
2 | 35 / 25 / 90 | Food and drink access
3 | 35 / 25 / 100 | Short, well-lit evening section and exit
4 | 25 / 20 / 65 | Easier week; equipment review
5 | 35 / 25 / 110 | Sheltered stop
6 | 40 / 25 / 120 | Familiar mixed terrain and crossings
7 | 40 / 25 / 130 | Evening section and familiar kit
8 | 25 / 20 / 80 | Easier week; readiness review
