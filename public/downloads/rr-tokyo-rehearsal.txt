Four-week urban endurance rehearsal

For walkers already comfortable with repeated 90-minute or longer outings. This block rehearses logistics, not 100 km readiness. Set L to your recent repeatable long walk; use easy effort and familiar shoes throughout.

Week 1: two 30–40 minute easy walks; one familiar long walk at L. Record moving and elapsed time.
Week 2: two easy walks of the same length; repeat L with planned refill and foot checks.
Week 3: keep the same weekly time; move one short session to a well-lit evening route with a transport exit. On the long walk, rehearse pauses and route changes.
Week 4: reduce long outing to roughly two-thirds of L, keep easy short walks, review footwear and exit plan. Do not add missed work.
