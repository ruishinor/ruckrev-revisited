RUCKING REVOLUTION — Twelve weeks of long urban walking

Starting requirement: repeatable comfortable two-hour walks and regular shorter outings. This is a foundation block, not a complete 100 km training plan.

Week | Easy walk / strength / long walk (minutes) | What to rehearse
1 | 40 / 25 / 110 | Moving versus elapsed time
2 | 40 / 25 / 120 | Socks, lacing and pressure points
3 | 45 / 25 / 130 | Refills and food stop
4 | 30 / 20 / 85 | Easier week; recovery
5 | 45 / 25 / 135 | Repeatable long outing
6 | 45 / 25 / 145 | Route alternatives and exits
7 | 45 / 25 / 155 | Familiar hard surfaces
8 | 30 / 20 / 100 | Easier week; footwear review
9 | 45 / 25 / 160 | Finish and transport buffer
10 | 45 / 25 / 170 | Food and refill plan
11 | 45 / 25 / 180 | Comfortable first hour; compare last hour
12 | 30 / 20 / 110 | Easier week; reassess the remaining gap
